Circle to Search

Ctrl+Shift+C

Freeze your entire screen, drag a box around anything — text, an image, an error — and Google Lens results slide out from the right with read-aloud buttons.

  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+C — the screen freezes and dims
  2. Click and drag a rectangle around what you want to search (or just click to search the whole screen)
  3. Results open on the right — click 🔊 to hear any text read aloud
  4. Esc or a click on the frozen screen closes everything
✦

Hold to Gemini

Alt+Space

A floating Gemini prompt, anywhere, anytime. Ask questions, attach a screenshot of what's on screen, and watch the answer stream in live.

  1. Press Alt+Space — the Gemini palette slides up
  2. Type your question, then press Enter
  3. Want Gemini to see your screen? Click 📎 and select a region first
  4. Alt+Space or Esc hides it — chats stay in your Google history
✨ System tray: look for the sparkle near the clock (check the ^ overflow). Right-click it for Settings, updates and Quit.
⌨ Change shortcuts anytime in Settings — including Ctrl+, to open this window.
🔐 Sign-in happens in your real browser (Edge/Chrome) — Google never blocks it, and the app picks the session up automatically.
