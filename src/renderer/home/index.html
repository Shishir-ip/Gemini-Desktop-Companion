Gemini Desktop Companion

Your screen, one shortcut away from AI.

Circle anything to search it with Google Lens — or summon Gemini over any app.

Checking Gemini connection… Gemini works with a free API key from Google AI Studio.

Circle to Search

Ctrl+Shift+C

Your screen freezes with an edge glow — drag a box around anything and Google Lens results slide out from the right.

  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+C — the glow wakes instantly
  2. Drag around what you want to search (or click for the whole screen)
  3. Results open on the right — ask Gemini, copy or save the crop
  4. Esc or a click outside closes everything

Hold to Gemini

Alt+Space

A floating Gemini panel, anywhere, anytime. Ask questions, attach what's on screen, and watch answers stream in live.

  1. Press Alt+Space — the panel pops in
  2. Type your question, then press Enter
  3. Click the clip to attach a screen region first
  4. Esc minimizes it — your conversation keeps its context
✨ System tray: look for the sparkle near the clock (check the ^ overflow). Right-click it for Settings, updates and Quit.
⌨ Change shortcuts anytime in Settings — including Ctrl+, to open this window.
